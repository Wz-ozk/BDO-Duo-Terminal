How this is calculated

Model: pri costs 2 × base value on every attempt, win or lose — lost entirely if pri fails, at the pri success rate. If pri succeeds, additionally pay the pri entry fee (fee unit × 83) and stake one more base value to attempt duo. If duo then fails, that pri cost, pri entry fee, and duo stake are all lost, and the cycle restarts at pri. If duo succeeds, additionally pay the duo entry fee (fee unit × 406) and receive the duo value. The duo value you set is pre-tax — the same way the market shows it — and the calculator taxes it internally only for the after-tax figures. Breakeven ratio after tax = ((2 + pri rate) / final success chance + fee cost / base value) ÷ 0.85. Since a fresh pri attempt is only ever free of sunk cost at the very start of a cycle, expected outcome after tax = (duo value × 0.85) − expected total cost to an eventual success. Tax is fixed at 15%. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere or saved between opens.
